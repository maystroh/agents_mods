# Brain dump

When the user asks to shape a rough idea, or sends a clearly exploratory prompt with several intertwined requests, offer a brief with four parts: **the job**, **the why**, **guardrails**, and **done means**. Preserve the user's intent, terminology, and concrete details. Do not invent requirements.

Ask at most three short questions about gaps that would materially change the work. If the answers are not needed to make progress, mark the uncertainty and continue with a stated assumption. If the user wants the work done now, do it; do not require a separate approval of the brief.

Show the shaped brief in chat so the user can reuse or correct it. Codex cannot put replacement text back into the unsent composer through a skill.
